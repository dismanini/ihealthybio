▶️ Step 01: Body Data & Glucose Mode
Enter Height (cm) and Weight (kg) to enable the Blood Pressure algorithm (Hung 2025)[cite: 1, 4]. Choose between Fasting and Post-Meal glucose modes to apply accurate threshold references[cite: 1, 4].
▶️ Step 02: Camera Initialization
Click the ▶️ OPEN CAMERA button[cite: 3]. Grant camera permissions to initialize MediaPipe Face Landmarker tracking at ~30 FPS[cite: 3, 4].
▶️ Step 03: Recording & ROI Signal Capture
Position your face inside the bounding box[cite: 4]. Select duration (15s, 20s, or 30s) and click RECORD. High-precision CHROM+POS algorithms blend color channels to capture blood volume pulses[cite: 4].
▶️ Step 04: Real-time Metric Readouts
Once completed, all 13 physiological indicators automatically populate across the dashboard cards, including Heart Rate, BP, SpO₂, Stress Index, and HRV Score[cite: 4].
