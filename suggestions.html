Face Position

Keep your face correctly positioned during scanning for better image quality.

  • Look directly toward the camera.
  • Keep your face inside the scanning area.
  • Avoid extreme head angles.

Improve Scan Quality

Good lighting and camera positioning can improve facial landmark detection.

  • Use sufficient lighting.
  • Keep the camera lens clean.
  • Avoid strong backlighting.

  • Drink fluids regularly.

  • Consider your climate and activity level.

Important Health Disclaimer

iHealthyBio facial AI analysis and wellness suggestions are intended for informational, educational and demonstration purposes. They are not a medical diagnosis and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice.

Facial appearance alone cannot reliably diagnose a disease or determine a person's health condition. Heart rate, SpO₂, blood pressure, temperature and other vital signs require appropriate validated sensors, devices and/or algorithms.
