About us

Innovating in the field of computer vision and machine learning since 2016.

Our vision is to enable the unimaginable with computer vision technology created in a harmonic workplace. To do that, we keep developing our knowledge and skills in the fields of computer vision, machine learning, computer graphics and general computing, and using this critical mass of knowledge and expertise to deliver world-class products and services to our clients.

The beginning of our story

Founded in New Jersey,USA

Founded by computer scientists, ETouchUS Pvt Ltd promotes research collaboration with academic institutions to this day.

2002
Getting started

Face and character animation

The company’s founders were among the main contributors to the MPEG-4 Face and Body Animation International Standard, so it’s no wonder that our first focus was on face and character animation.

2008
Adapting to the market

Face tracking

ETouchUS Pvt Ltd has quickly adapted to strong market demands and focused on face tracking and analysis.

2010
New offices

Subsidiary in Zagreb, Croatia

Our new office was opened in Zagreb, where we’ve soon gathered an amazing team of experienced engineers

2015
Creating a specialized division

Automotive division

A new division of our company was formed for the purposes of exclusive collaboration with a major automotive company.

2016
Strong organic growth

Consistently among the fastest-growing companies

We have grown rapidly in the last few years, and currently have 180 engineers in our ranks who, together with our core scientific team, are making sure ETouchUS Pvt Ltd stays at the forefront of computer vision technology.

2022
Innovating in beauty AI

Our beauty tech brand

Arbelle, our new beauty tech brand, was launched in 2023 to provide cutting-edge beauty AR and AI solutions. Today, it is already powering some of the leading cosmetic brands.

2023
On-demand R&D teams

R&D lab for leading companies

For over two decades, we have successfully delivered medium and large-scale custom development projects for industry-leading companies. In 2024, we established a dedicated R&D lab to meet the rising demand for our services.
